Core mechanics

Nearly everything in the game runs on one loop. Learn it once and you can follow any table.

The d20 loop

1

Say what you try

You describe an action, such as leaping a pit. The Dungeon Master (the game's narrator) decides if the outcome is uncertain.

2

Roll and add

Roll a d20 and add the modifier from the ability that fits the action.

3

Beat the target

If your total meets or beats the Difficulty Class (DC) set by the Dungeon Master, you succeed.

Ability modifiers

Each ability has a score. The score gives a modifier, which is the number you actually add to a roll.

Advantage and disadvantage

Advantage

Roll two d20s and keep the higher one. It applies when the situation helps you, like attacking a distracted guard.

Disadvantage

Roll two d20s and keep the lower one. It applies when something hinders you, like fighting while blinded.
